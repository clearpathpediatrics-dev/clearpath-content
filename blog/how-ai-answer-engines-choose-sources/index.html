Industry Content Playbooks

How AI Answer Engines Decide Which Sources to Cite

July 25, 2026 · 6 min read

AI search optimizationanswer engine optimizationAEOPerplexityAI Overviews

When someone asks an AI assistant a question about your industry, it answers using a handful of sources. Not ten blue links — usually three to six pages, sometimes fewer. Being one of them is a different problem from ranking first, and the businesses that understand the difference are quietly collecting traffic that never shows up as a classic search impression.

Retrieval is not ranking

A traditional search engine orders pages. An answer engine does something else: it retrieves passages, evaluates whether they answer the question, and synthesises a response. That means a page ranking eighth can be cited while the page ranking first is skipped — because citation depends on whether a specific passage cleanly answers the specific question.

What this changes in practice

Optimising for retrieval means optimising at the passage level. Each section of your page should be able to stand alone and answer something completely, without requiring the three paragraphs above it for context.

How do I make my content more likely to be cited by AI?

Answer the question in the first sentence beneath the heading, then support it. Answer engines favour passages where the claim and its context sit together in a compact block. A section that opens with two paragraphs of throat-clearing before reaching the point is far less quotable than one that leads with the answer and elaborates afterwards.

  • Phrase H2 headings as the question a person would actually type or say.
  • Put the complete answer in the first sentence after the heading.
  • Keep each answer self-contained — no "as mentioned above".
  • Include specifics: numbers, steps, named conditions. Vague prose does not get quoted.

Does schema markup matter for AI search?

It helps, but less than clear writing does. Structured data makes your entities and relationships explicit, which reduces the work a model has to do to understand what your business is and what a page covers. FAQPage and Organization markup are worth implementing. They are not a substitute for content that answers the question directly.

A short audit you can run today

  1. List the ten questions your buyers ask before purchasing.
  2. For each, ask an AI assistant that question and note which sources it cites.
  3. Read the cited passages. Note their structure — heading phrasing, answer position, specificity.
  4. Compare against your own page on that topic. Usually the gap is structural, not topical.

The pattern is consistent: cited passages answer immediately, completely, and specifically. That is the whole trick, and it is unglamorous.
