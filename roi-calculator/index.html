Common questions

Where do these numbers come from?

Every figure is either one you typed or arithmetic on the figures you typed. We do not model a result for you, because we would be guessing at your market, your close rate and your capacity.

Why does it lead with break-even instead of projected leads?

Because break-even is the only number we can state honestly. What it takes to cover the cost is arithmetic. What you will actually get depends on your market, your competition and your close rate — so that part is a slider you control, not a promise we make.

How long before it breaks even in practice?

Published content compounds rather than switching on. Most of what we publish takes one to two quarters to rank and then keeps working. Plan on the first two quarters being investment and judge it from there.

What if it never brings a single extra customer?

Then it was the wrong answer for your market and you should stop. It is month to month for that reason, and everything published stays on your domain and remains yours.
