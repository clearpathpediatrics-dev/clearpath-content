Questions small businesses ask

Is content marketing worth it for a small business?

It is worth it where your buyers research before purchasing, which covers most considered services. It is worth less for pure impulse categories with no research phase. The honest test is simple: if you have ever had a customer say they found you while looking into the problem, content works in your market.

How much does content marketing cost for a small business?

Agency retainers commonly run into the thousands per month, which prices most small businesses out. Deployment tiers here run $199 to $499 per month, month to month, with everything published staying yours permanently.

How long before a small business sees results?

It depends on your market and how competitive your category is. Some see movement within weeks; most compound over one to two quarters. The advantage of starting small and early is that the compounding has already begun by the time you actually need the leads.
