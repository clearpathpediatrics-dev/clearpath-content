What lands on your domain

01

Articles published to your own domain

Between 8 and 22 long-form articles a month depending on tier, published directly to your site. Not a portal, not a content library you have to migrate — your domain, your URLs, your asset.

02

Coverage that only widens

State is tracked permanently, so topics never repeat and coverage expands every cycle. Month three covers ground month one did not, by design.

03

Structure, not a pile of pages

Articles are organised into topical groups and interlinked with each other, so the body of work reads as a coherent library rather than scattered posts. Structure is what separates content that accumulates authority from content that just exists.

04

Written in your voice

Conditioned on your real terminology, offers, market and positioning — plus an explicit list of phrases you never want to see. Standing preferences are permanent, not re-briefed each time.

05

A cadence that does not lapse

Publication runs on schedule every week without exception. Sustained cadence is one of the strongest trust signals a domain emits, and the first thing every manual effort abandons.

06

Built for AI answer engines too

Articles are structured so assistants like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews can surface them directly. A growing share of buyer research now happens there, and most competitors are not structured for it at all.

Common questions

How much of my time does this take?

About thirty minutes once, to configure your deployment. After that your involvement is optional — review drafts if you want to, or let it run. Most clients move to full automation within about two weeks.

Do I need to supply topics or briefs?

No. Identifying what your market searches for is part of the program. You are welcome to request specific topics, and standing requests are added to your configuration permanently.

What if I already have a blog?

Existing content stays where it is. New work is published alongside it and interlinked with what you already have, so the existing library gets reinforced rather than orphaned.
